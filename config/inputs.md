## A note on the per-gene ASE statistic

Two quantities appear in the data and they are not interchangeable:

* `a` in the per-sample `{T,N}{ID}_ase_results.csv` files is the per-gene, phase-marginalised
  beta-binomial estimate. This is the correct statistic for gene-level analyses.
* Per-SNP `|ref_ratio − 0.5|` is a coarse per-site quantity. It is appropriate for site-level
  analyses such as directional concordance, but it is not a per-gene effect size.

Analyses that aggregate to the gene level use `a`.
